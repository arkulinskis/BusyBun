How to play

You are a little bao on a culinary journey. Each territory is overrun by kitchen pests. Clear the levels to free it!

WASD / ←↑↓→Roll around (on phones: drag on the left side) Space / ShiftSteam Dash: bonk pests, smash crates, wreck nests (on phones: the 💨 button; hold it 2 seconds to drag it where you like) Esc / PPause

In each level: collect the meat, help the neighbours with their errands, destroy the pest nests, then hop into the bamboo steamer.

Stars: ⭐ finish · ⭐ collect every ingredient · ⭐ beat the par time.

Bonuses glow in bubbles: 🧲 magnet, 🛡️ shield, 💰 coin bag, 🌟 Star Anise (bonk everything you touch), ⏳ sand timer (stops the clock), 🍵 jasmine tea (speed).

Hot stoves hurt. Touch an 🎛️ oven knob to turn every stove off, or a ⏲️ kitchen timer to turn them off for 12 seconds.

Lost? The Journey tab always shows your next level at the top, and every world with how far you got.

Between levels: fill your Pantry, cook recipes in the Kitchen for permanent perks, buy hats, open the daily market box, and hunt for golden chopsticks and Grandma's postcards.
